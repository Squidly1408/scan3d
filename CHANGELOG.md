## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-24

### Added
- Real-time point-cloud capture from ARKit (LiDAR scene mesh or feature points) and ARCore (Depth API or feature points).
- 2 cm voxel de-duplication with a 2M-point cap.
- Live mini-map with the camera trail.
- 3D viewer with orbit, zoom, pan and a top-down floor-plan view.
- Binary PLY export with height colours, through the system share sheet.
- Brand: point-cloud mark, dot-matrix wordmark, app icons and README banner, all generated by `tool/brand/generate.py`.
- Documentation in `docs/`, and CI that runs analysis, tests and Android and iOS builds.

## Roadmap

Ideas, roughly in order of value. Nothing here is promised.

- **Real colour.** Sample the camera image at each point's projection and replace the height colour.
- **Save and load scans.** Keep clouds on the device and reopen them in the viewer.
- **Mesh export on LiDAR.** Write the ARKit mesh as OBJ or USDZ, not only its vertices.
- **Continue a scan.** Use ARKit `ARWorldMap` / ARCore Cloud Anchors to relocalise and add to an earlier session.
- **Show points in the Android preview.** Draw the captured cloud over the camera in GL, the way iOS does.
- **Measurement.** Tap two points in the viewer to get a distance.
